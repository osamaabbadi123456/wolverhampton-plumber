# Wolverhampton Plumber Short Video Scripts

Use vertical 1080x1920 format. Videos in this folder are silent draft reels generated from the pin artwork. Add voiceover or captions if desired.

## Video 1 — Toilet Water Rising
If your toilet water rises after flushing, do not keep flushing. Wait and check whether the water level drops. Then note whether it is only the toilet, whether the bath or basin is slow too, and whether water is close to overflowing. For Wolverhampton enquiries, send your area, the issue, and how urgent it feels.

## Video 2 — Slow Kitchen Sink
A slow kitchen sink is not always a full blockage. Check whether water stands in the sink, whether there is gurgling, any smell, any water under the cupboard, and whether a dishwasher is connected. Send those details with your Wolverhampton area.

## Video 3 — Low Pressure From One Tap
If only one tap has weak flow, say which tap it is. Check whether the problem is hot water, cold water or both, kitchen or bathroom, one tap or several, and sudden or gradual. These details make the enquiry clearer.

## Video 4 — Water Under the Sink
Water under a sink can come from different places. Check the tap base, waste pipe, supply pipe, cabinet floor, and whether it leaks only when the tap runs. If water is near electrics, stay safe and mention it clearly.

## Video 5 — Shower Drain Smells
A shower drain smell is easier to explain if you check the pattern. Does water drain slowly, is there gurgling, does the smell appear after use, and are the bath or basin affected too? Send those details with your Wolverhampton area.
